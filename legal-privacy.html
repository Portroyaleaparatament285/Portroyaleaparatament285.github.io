Legal & Privacy

Clear information about the accommodation, this website and how personal information is handled.

Website purpose and reservations

This website presents Port Royale Apartment 285, provides practical information for guests and publishes the independent Tenerife Guide editorial project.

The website does not accept reservations, payments or create customer accounts. Booking buttons lead to Booking.com, where availability, prices, reservations, payments, cancellations and platform terms are managed.

Personal data

For personal information sent directly to us, the data controller is Cismaru Petre Catalin, the owner and website operator identified on this page.

There are no contact forms, newsletter subscriptions or analytics tools on this website. The website does not sell personal data.

This website does not use automated decision-making or profiling.

If you contact us voluntarily by email, telephone or WhatsApp, the information you provide is used only to answer your enquiry, manage matters related to your requested or existing stay and meet applicable legal obligations. Depending on the situation, processing is based on steps requested before a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests in communicating with guests, or a legal obligation.

Personal information is retained only for as long as needed for those purposes and for any applicable legal or accounting requirements.

Local storage and cookies

This website does not use advertising, profiling or analytics cookies. On the 404 error page, choosing a language stores that selection in your browser's local storage for that page. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Because the site does not use non-essential tracking technologies, it does not display a cookie-consent banner.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Details are available in the GitHub Pages data collection information.

The site contains links to external services such as Booking.com, WhatsApp, Facebook, Google Maps, TripAdvisor and Cicar. These services are not embedded in the site. When you choose an external link, the privacy policy and terms of that provider apply.

Messages sent by email or WhatsApp are processed through the communication provider you choose, such as Yahoo Mail or WhatsApp/Meta. Those providers process the information needed to deliver the communication under their own privacy terms and may process it outside the European Economic Area using the safeguards described in those terms. Booking.com is responsible for the personal information it collects on its own platform.

Your privacy rights
