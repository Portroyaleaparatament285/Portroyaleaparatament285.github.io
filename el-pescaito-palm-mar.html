One Last Taste of Tenerife

A seafood surprise in Palm-Mar. Sometimes, the best memories come from small detours.

Catalin C. · Visit and original photography · 18 October 2026

We were on our way from Los Cristianos to Tenerife South Airport when we decided to make one last stop in Palm-Mar.

No elaborate plans. Just the wish to enjoy a good meal before saying goodbye to the island.

And that is how we discovered El Pescaíto.

A terrace, and a little time to spare

A simple terrace beneath a blue awning, hanging ferns, a relaxed atmosphere and the unmistakable feeling of everyday life in Tenerife.

A basket of fresh bread arrived with three colourful sauces, followed by a cold Dorada beer. Nothing complicated. Just the pleasure of slowing down for a little longer.

Prawns and coquinas

Then came the seafood.

The piruletas de gambas — delicious prawns served on little wooden skewers — were simply the best we had tasted. Golden, beautifully prepared and absolutely worth the stop.

The coquinas al ajillo, tiny clams in a fragrant garlic sauce, were equally memorable. Exceptional, in fact. The kind of dish that makes you want to enjoy every last drop of sauce with a piece of bread.

Sometimes, the most wonderful meals happen in the simplest places.

No elaborate setting, no special occasion. Just a quiet terrace, a cold beer and two unforgettable seafood dishes.

The small detours that stay with you

What began as a small detour before the airport became one of those unexpected moments that stay with you long after a trip ends.

If you find yourself travelling between Los Cristianos and Tenerife South Airport, and have a little time to spare, Palm-Mar might just surprise you.

And if you stop at El Pescaíto, don’t miss the prawns on skewers and the coquinas.
