Cose Nostre, a little taste of Sicily in Tenerife

Savoury pastries, an inviting counter and a small Sicilian discovery in southern Tenerife.
Catalin C. · Visit and original photography · October 2026

There is something wonderful about discovering a place that makes you forget what you originally went looking for.

Perhaps it was just a quick breakfast. A coffee. Something small to eat before continuing the day.

And then you notice a display filled with golden pastries, savoury breads, miniature pizzas and beautifully arranged desserts.

Suddenly, a simple stop becomes an experience worth remembering.

That is exactly the kind of discovery we love in Tenerife.

And Cose Nostre – Sicily Food, an informal Sicilian café and bakery in southern Tenerife, near Los Cristianos, is a particularly delicious example.

A little corner of Sicily in Tenerife

Los Cristianos has a distinctly international character.

Spanish cafés sit alongside restaurants inspired by cuisines from across Europe and beyond. And among these everyday places, you can occasionally discover something that feels rooted in a very particular food tradition.

Cose Nostre brings the flavours of Sicily to Tenerife.

Its identity is proudly Italian, but the selection goes beyond the familiar idea of an Italian coffee shop serving croissants and espresso.

This is a place where sweet pastries share the counter with savoury specialities, filled breads and small pizza-style creations.

The concept reflects a familiar Sicilian tradition: the rosticceria, where generously varied savoury snacks and baked goods are part of everyday life.

Nothing needs to be particularly formal.

You look at what is on offer, choose what appeals to you and enjoy a little taste of Italy.

The irresistible pleasure of choosing from the counter

One of the most enjoyable things about Cose Nostre is simply looking at the display.

Rows of golden, rounded breads sit alongside sausage-filled pastries, savoury rolls and small pizzas.

Some are topped with tomato, cheese and slices of cured meat. Others are enclosed in beautifully browned dough, leaving their fillings to be discovered.

The sweet selection is equally inviting.

Behind the glass are delicate pastries filled with cream, fruit tartlets topped with berries and other treats inspired by Italian baking traditions.

It is the kind of counter that makes choosing just one thing unexpectedly difficult.

And unlike a menu filled with elaborate descriptions, everything is there to see.

The photographs from our visit capture that abundance particularly well.

What we tried — and why we loved it

Of course, an attractive display means very little if the food itself disappoints.

Fortunately, our experience at Cose Nostre was quite the opposite.

We tried savoury specialities, including a golden filled pastry and a small pizza-style bread topped with tomato, melted cheese and thin slices of cured meat.

The products we tasted were exceptional.

They turned what could have been an ordinary snack into a genuinely memorable food stop.

What impressed us was the pleasure of the food itself. These were uncomplicated things to eat, yet the quality made them stand out.

No elaborate dining room, no theatrical presentation.

Just food that made us genuinely glad we had stopped.

We also photographed the impressive range of sweet pastries and other baked goods in the display, although our recommendation is based on the products we actually tasted.

That distinction matters to us: the most useful travel recommendations come from real experiences.

Good food doesn't have to be expensive

Another pleasant part of our visit was the pricing.

We found the prices good, particularly considering how much we enjoyed the products.

We would not describe Cose Nostre as a fine-dining destination, and that would miss the point entirely.

Its appeal lies in something more everyday: being able to stop for a tasty breakfast, a savoury snack or a casual bite without turning it into an expensive occasion.

For travellers exploring Los Cristianos, this is exactly the sort of place worth knowing about.

Menus and prices can change, of course, so it is always sensible to check the current selection.

Breakfast, a quick lunch or something sweet?

Cose Nostre is especially appealing because it does not need to fit just one moment of the day.

You might visit for an Italian-style breakfast, a coffee and pastry, something savoury around lunchtime or a small sweet treat.

The wide selection makes it interesting for visitors who enjoy discovering food beyond the standard tourist menu.

It is also an opportunity to experience a little of Sicily's culinary identity without leaving Tenerife.

If you are wondering where to eat in Los Cristianos and would rather explore an inviting food counter than commit to a formal restaurant meal, Cose Nostre is worth considering.

Where to find Cose Nostre

Cose Nostre is located at Calle Ramona Martín Artista 1, 38650 Arona, in southern Tenerife, near Los Cristianos. It is an appealing stop for Sicilian savoury specialities, Italian pastries and a relaxed coffee break.

Check the current opening hours and menu before visiting.

Our verdict: a Sicilian discovery worth remembering

Would we recommend Cose Nostre? Absolutely.

We were impressed by the exceptional quality of the products we tried, the variety on display and the reasonable prices.

But there is another reason we wanted to write about it.

Places like this remind us that exploring a destination is not only about beaches, famous viewpoints or carefully planned excursions.

It is also about the little discoveries made between those experiences.

A bakery you had not planned to visit.

A display that makes you stop.

Something delicious you did not expect.

And the pleasure of realising that an ordinary moment has become one of the nicest parts of your day.

Near Los Cristianos, we found a little taste of Sicily.

And it was a discovery worth sharing.
