Pool & House Rules

Pool Rules

  • Pool hours: Summer 09:00–20:00; Winter 09:00–19:00
  • Maximum capacity: 20 persons
  • No diving or running around the pool area
  • No glass, bottles, food or music near the pool
  • Music only with headphones
  • No reserving sunbeds
  • No inflatables, floats or balls in the pool
  • No animals in the pool area
  • Children wearing nappies must use swim nappies
  • Children must be supervised at all times
  • Please shower before entering the pool
  • Do not walk or play in planted garden areas
  • Do not feed animals
  • Please respect the facilities and other residents

House Rules

  • Please keep noise levels low and respect neighbours.
  • Parties or events are strictly not allowed.
  • Household waste must be disposed of only in the designated refuse rooms.
  • Please keep all common areas clean.
  • Do not hang towels or clothes over terrace railings or in common areas.
