AboutTenerife Guide

Catalin C., creator and editor of Tenerife Guide, an independent editorial project by Port Royale Apartment 285 in Los Cristianos, Tenerife.

Tenerife Guide was created to help visitors discover the island with more context and less noise. It brings together carefully selected places, practical travel information and personal observations — from well-known landmarks to quieter corners of the island that are worth taking the time to explore.

The aim is simple: to offer clear, honest and useful guidance for travellers who want to understand Tenerife a little better and experience the island at their own pace.

— Catalin C.
